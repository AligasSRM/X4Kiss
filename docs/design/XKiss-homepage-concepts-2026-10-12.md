## Proposed independent V4 concept
- Desktop-first video platform homepage, not a marketing/landing page.
- Top navigation bar: XKiss logo, primary navigation, search, language, location, and account controls.
- Second navigation bar: categories, sorting, filters, and view controls.
- Compact settings/navigation area on the left side of the desktop layout.
- Settings expand/collapse with a chevron; when expanded, grouped settings appear below the settings heading.
- Main content area: four-column video grid with video cards and save/bookmark controls.
- Pagination and help/settings links.
- Current thumbnail imagery in concept designs is placeholder-only and must not be represented as actual XKiss video content.
